About RitaGreen
RitaGreen Healthcare Services Ltd has grown into four businesses that share one purpose: looking after people through every part of their day. We staff and run care, we train the next wave of carers through our academy, we keep buildings clean and safe, and we guard the places people live and work. Today each arm runs on its own website and its own paperwork. RitaGreenOS is our own private platform that brings all four onto one system, so the office runs the whole group from a single screen. It is built for RitaGreen alone, for our staff and our clients, not as a product for anyone else.
Our Mission
RitaGreen exists to keep people safe, cared for and supported through life by bringing trained, checked and trusted staff to the doorsteps, buildings and events that need them.
Our Vision
A London where a care home, a landlord or an event organiser can call one trusted name for staff, training, cleaning or security and know every worker is qualified, checked and ready.
What We Build
RitaGreenOS is the operating system for the whole group: one place to onboard clients and workers, schedule shifts, track every certificate, run the academy, raise invoices and get paid, across all four divisions.
