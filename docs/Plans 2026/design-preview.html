1. Palette

Colour and contrast

A single green family carries the brand, on a warm paper background. Ochre is kept for small highlights only. Each swatch shows its contrast ratio against white and against ink (#1C221B), with the WCAG 2.2 grade for normal text: AAA is 7:1 or more, AA is 4.5:1, "Large" means headings and bold text at 24px or more only.

"Fill" and "Line" mean the colour is for backgrounds or borders only, never for text on that pairing. Ochre falls just short of AA for small text on either background, so it is used for dots, borders and labels set in a darker ochre (#7A5115). The four status pairs below all pass AA: Valid 5.3:1, Due soon 5.2:1, Expired 6.2:1, Awaiting review 8.5:1.
