## Post templates (four layouts)

Service introduction, vacancy, training date, team story. Same green, same type, real photo where possible. Sizes: 1080 by 1350 (portrait feed), 1080 by 1920 (stories, reels, TikTok), 1200 by 627 (LinkedIn link image).

## First four weeks

Two posts a week, reused across platforms. One short video a fortnight if genuine footage exists.

| Week | Post one | Post two |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Meet RitaGreen and the people behind it | Four services, one team, and how to reach each |
| 2 | How to request healthcare staff | How to apply for a role (link to careers) |
| 3 | Cleaning contracts: what we need for a quote | A training session or trainer introduction |
| 4 | Security: tell us about your site | A team story or applicant FAQ |

Sample client post: *Planning cover for your team? Tell us the roles, dates and location and we will come back to you with checked, trained staff. Request staff on our website.*

Sample recruitment post: *We are hiring support workers and carers in London. Read the role, then apply in a few minutes on your phone. Please do not send documents by message; we will ask for them securely later.*

## Measuring

Monthly: enquiries and applications that came from each platform (UTM tags on every link), response time to messages, useful conversations. Followers are secondary. No paid adverts until the website forms and follow-up are working.
